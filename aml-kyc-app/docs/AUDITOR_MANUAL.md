# Auditor User Manual

## Signing in
Open the Web App URL with your firm Google account. If you see **Access Denied**, ask the MLRO/System Administrator to add or reactivate you.

## Adding a company
*Companies -> Add Company*. Provide the legal name, country, and at least a QFC number or CR number. The system checks for duplicates and creates the company's Google Drive folder automatically. A KYC review (Draft) is created for you.

## Company profile tabs
| Tab | What you do |
|---|---|
| Overview | Company details, KYC summary, warnings, mandatory-document progress. Edit while the record is not locked |
| Engagements | Add several engagements (type, dates, partner/manager, assigned auditor). Risk level is set by the MLRO |
| UBOs | Add every UBO with ownership %, PEP/sanctions/adverse-media screening results, source of wealth/funds, verification. QID/passport are visible to you but masked for viewers |
| Ownership | Enter shareholders; for a holder of an intermediate company choose "Holds shares in". The chain and effective % are calculated; warnings show gaps (not 100%, untraced entities, individuals above the threshold who are not UBOs). Upload the ownership chart here |
| Documents | Upload, view, download, replace, delete (your own). Show version history to see superseded files |
| KYC Review | Work through the checklist with comments, then **Submit for review** |
| Risk Assessment | Answer each risk factor (use *Apply suggestions* as a starting point) and save. The MLRO sets the final rating |
| Audit History | Everything that happened to this company |

## Uploading documents
1. Documents tab -> **Upload document**; choose the type, the person/engagement it relates to (e.g. the UBO for a passport), the expiry date (drives alerts) and the file (allowed types and size are shown).
2. A new upload of a single-instance type (e.g. CR, Trade License) automatically marks the previous one **Superseded** - nothing is deleted. For per-person types (QID, Passport) the same person's previous file is superseded; other types (bank statements etc.) keep all files.
3. Use **Upload replacement** on a row to replace that specific document.
4. Only the MLRO/DMLRO can mark documents Verified/Rejected. If a document is rejected or a replacement is requested, upload a new one.

## Submitting for review
Complete the checklist and risk assessment, check the *Mandatory documents* list is green, then **Submit for review**. The record is locked while under review. If the MLRO rejects it, read the comment, fix the issues (it is unlocked), and **Resubmit**. You cannot approve your own submission.

## After approval
Approved records are locked. To change anything (new UBO, updated details) choose **Start new review cycle** with a reason, make the changes, and submit again. You can always upload renewed documents (for example a new trade license) without a new cycle.

## Deleting
You can delete engagements, UBOs, shareholders and documents that **you** created while the record is unlocked; a reason is required and the action is audited. Companies can only be deleted by the MLRO/DMLRO.

## Tips
- Global search (top bar) finds companies by name, QFC, CR, UBO, engagement, auditor or risk rating.
- *Expiring Documents* lists items due for renewal.
- Yellow banners are warnings, not errors - they highlight items to fix before submission.
